# Mortgage Loan Comparison

A small, beginner-friendly tool for comparing two fixed-rate mortgage options side by side:

- Monthly principal and interest payment
- Monthly property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage insurance
- Total estimated monthly payment
- Cash to close
- Clear highlights for the lower monthly payment and lower cash-to-close amount

## Open the calculator

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No installation or internet connection is required.

Enter the loan details and estimated costs for Option A and Option B, then select **Compare loan options**. Annual property taxes and homeowners insurance are divided by 12; monthly mortgage insurance is added directly to the payment. Cash to close is entered as an estimate from the lender because it can include a down payment, closing costs, credits, and other items.

> This calculator is an estimate. Actual payments may include HOA fees or other costs, and taxes and insurance premiums can change.
