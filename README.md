# Mortgage Payment Calculator

A small, beginner-friendly web calculator for estimating a fixed-rate mortgage's:

- Monthly principal and interest payment
- Monthly property taxes, homeowners insurance, and mortgage insurance
- Total estimated monthly payment
- Total of all payments
- Total interest paid over the life of the loan

## Open the calculator

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No installation or internet connection is required.

Enter the loan details and estimated taxes and insurance, then select **Calculate payment**. Annual property taxes and homeowners insurance are divided by 12; monthly mortgage insurance is added directly to the payment.

> This calculator is an estimate. Actual payments may include HOA fees or other costs, and taxes and insurance premiums can change.
